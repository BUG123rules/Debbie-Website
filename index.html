Welcome

Hi, and I’m glad you landed on this page. Sometimes, the only thing harder than reaching out for support is doing nothing.

What I mean is that it can take enormous courage to reach out, and if you are here, you may be considering support for yourself or someone you want to refer for trustworthy care.

I welcome you to meet with me for a brief, 15-minute online consultation at no charge, just to see how it feels to work together.

I can be especially helpful if you are a helper too. I see nurses, teachers, therapists, creatives, caregivers, and many others.

We can work with many concerns together, including relationship issues, boundaries, compassion fatigue, life transitions, past trauma, and creative expression. I also have extensive training in supporting folks with eating and body image issues.

Maybe you don’t see your issue listed here. Let’s chat, and I will tell you honestly if it’s something I am qualified to support you with. I enthusiastically welcome all genders, sexualities, and body sizes. I am curious about you and what brings you here, and I welcome the opportunity to see things from where you are as you move toward feeling more healed and whole in your own process.

Training & Professional Background

I am trained by the IFS Institute in Internal Family Systems (IFS) therapy and will be trained in EMDR at the beginning of 2026. I have worked relationally and from a client-centered focus since 2008. I have an M.A. in Marriage and Family Therapy from Saybrook University in San Francisco, CA, and I am licensed in California and Oregon for psychotherapy sessions. If you are in another state or country and meet specific criteria, we can work together via private-pay coaching.

I’m happy to answer any questions you may have. I have worked in private practice, hospital, and university settings, but my favorite is meeting with individual adults online or in groups (and sometimes in person through groups, retreats, or workshops).

I also offer a few reduced-rate spots for students and new providers, but those slots are currently full. Please let me know if you would like to be added to the waitlist.

Out-of-network support

Additionally, I can provide out-of-network receipts (superbills) that you may submit to your insurance company for possible reimbursement, if you have appropriate out-of-network coverage on your current plan.
