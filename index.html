Welcome

Hi and I’m glad you landed on this page. Sometimes, the only thing harder than reaching out for support is nothing.

What I mean is that it can take enormous courage to reach out and if you are here, you may be considering support for yourself or someone you want to refer to for trustworthy care.

I welcome you to meet with me for a brief 15 minutes or so online to see how you feel about working together-no charge.

I can be helpful, particularly if you are a helper too. I see nurses, teachers, therapists, creatives, caregivers and many others.

We can work with all sorts of issues together like relationship concerns, boundaries, compassion fatigue, life transitions, past trauma, creative expression, and I have some pretty wonderful training in supporting folks with eating and body image issues as well.

Maybe you don’t see your issue listed here. Let’s chat and I will tell you honestly if it is something I am qualified to support you with. I enthusiastically welcome all genders, sexualities and body sizes. I am curious about you and what brings you and I welcome the opportunity to see things from where you are and will actively learn with you as you become more healed and whole in your own process.

Training

I am trained by the IFS institute in IFS therapy and will be trained in EMDR in the beginning of 2026. I have worked relationally and client centered focus since 2008. I have an M.A degree in Marriage and Family Therapy from Saybrook University in San Francisco, CA and am licensed in California & Oregon for Psychotherapy sessions. If you are in another state or country and meet specific criteria, we can do private pay coaching.

I am happy to answer any questions you may have. I have worked in private practice, in hospital and university settings but my favorite is to meet with individual adults online or in groups (sometimes I offer in person groups, retreats or workshops).
